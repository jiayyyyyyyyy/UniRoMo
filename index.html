UniRoMo: Rethinking Unified Robust Multimodal Learning via Consensus-Innovation Optimization

Yue Jiang1, Yunqiu Xu2, Wenchao Meng1, Qinmin Yang1, Jiming Chen1
1Zhejiang University   2National University of Singapore
Submitted

Abstract

Multimodal learning focuses on integrating information from vision, audition, and language for more accurate prediction, but low-quality modalities can significantly weaken model robustness. Existing methods mainly handle low-quality modalities through representation-level reconstruction or suppression, but often rely on complete or clean modality information and may introduce extra modules or remove useful semantics. More importantly, low-quality modalities not only degrade input representations, but also disturb cross-modal interactions and optimization dynamics, limiting the robustness of mainstream optimization paradigms such as joint and alternating training. To address these challenges, we view low-quality modality robustness from the perspective of shared semantic alignment and formulate it as semantic alignment during optimization, thereby avoiding the need for complete training modalities or heavy extra modules. To realize this formulation, we propose a Unified consensus-innovation framework for Robust Multimodal Optimization (UniRoMo), which introduces a unified optimization objective balancing task learning and consensus alignment. To solve this objective, UniRoMo employs two key mechanisms: (i) structure-guided dynamic consensus estimation, which constructs a reliable shared semantic anchor, and (ii) innovation-based anisotropic gradient modulation, which suppresses unreliable optimization directions according to long-term deviations from the consensus. Extensive experiments across traditional encoders and MLLM-based backbones show that UniRoMo consistently improves both optimization paradigms when learning from low-quality training data with missing, noisy, and hybrid modality degradations, while incurring only limited additional computational overhead. Overall, UniRoMo provides a unified and broadly applicable optimization framework for robust multimodal learning.
